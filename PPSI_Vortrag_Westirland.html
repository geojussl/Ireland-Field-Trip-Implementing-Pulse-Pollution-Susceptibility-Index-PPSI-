2. Methods: PPSI framework

\[ {\mathrm{PPSI}}= \sqrt[3]{ \color{#006837}{S} \cdot \color{#72368d}{M} \cdot \color{#45bcbb}{C} } \]

Equal-weighted geometric mean of three normalized components

S

Source presence

Where are agricultural sources present?

M

Mobilization potential

Can rainfall mobilize the material?

C

Hydrological connectivity

Can it reach the stream network?

5. Take-home messages

  • Heavy rainfall does not produce the same pollution response everywhere.
  • The PPSI consistently identifies priority catchments for monitoring after heavy rainfall. Event-based field measurements remain necessary to confirm actual pollution.
